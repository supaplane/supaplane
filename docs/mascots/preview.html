Palette

Role Hex Use
Navy field #163A5F Tile, eyes, smile, drawn arms, plane crease
Cream #EDE3C4 Body, legs, suitcase fill

Two colors. No third. No outline around the body — navy is the outline.

How to draw them

  • Body is one cream silhouette. Soft, slightly uneven is fine. That’s the life.
  • Face: two small dots, shallow smile. Never pupils, brows, or a filled mouth.
  • Arms that hold something are navy strokes on the body, not cream sausages sticking out.
  • A waving or walking arm can be cream, still part of the silhouette.
  • The paper plane on the chest is a navy line drawing of the official fold.
  • A suitcase is cream fill, same weight as the body. Handle can be a navy stroke.
  • Never bag and plane on the same figure.
  • Families mix shapes. Same face on every size.

How to make more

Prompt Imagine from one of these pictures. Ask for the same cream, same navy, same face. Change only the body type or the prop. Then pick the frame that still matches this sheet. Don’t vector-rebuild unless a pose has to loop in the app — and even then, trace this drawing, don’t invent a new one.
